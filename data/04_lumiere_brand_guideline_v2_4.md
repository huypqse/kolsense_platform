## Brand Identity Overview

**Company Name:** Lumière Collective
**Founded:** 2019, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam
**Brand Positioning:** Premium-accessible DTC beauty brand, SEA-born with Pan-Asian identity
**Operating Markets:** VN, TH, ID, SG, MY
**Flagship Product Lines:**
- Glow Serum Series (hero skincare line)
- Velvet Cushion Collection (color cosmetics)
- Clean Ritual Body Care (body and wellness)

### Brand Archetype
**Primary: The Creator** — Lumière Collective believes in the power of self-expression through beauty. We create products that enable individuals to craft their own rituals, their own glow, their own story.
**Secondary: The Lover** — We are deeply invested in the relationship between our customers and their skin. Beauty, for us, is an act of care and intimacy — not performance.

### Core Brand Pillars

| Pillar | Description |
|---|---|
| **Clean Beauty** | Every Lumière Collective product is free from parabens, sulfates, synthetic fragrance, and any ingredient not compatible with our halal-certified formulation standards. Clean is not a trend for us — it is the foundation. |
| **Cultural Pride** | We are proudly SEA-born. Our ingredients, our aesthetics, and our community reflect Southeast Asia's diversity, botanical heritage, and modern creative energy. We do not imitate — we originate. |
| **Accessible Luxury** | Premium beauty should not require a premium income. Lumière Collective is committed to delivering luxury formulation standards at mid-range price points, making high-efficacy skincare the norm, not the exception, for our communities. |

### Tagline Options

1. **"Your Glow, Your Story"** — Consumer-centric, empowering, platform-native
2. **"Born in SEA. Made for You."** — Origin-proud, community-first, regional identity
3. **"Glow Forward."** — Aspirational, momentum-driven (campaign alignment with Q2 2025 activation)

### Brand Personality Traits

| Trait | Description |
|---|---|
| **Radiant** | Warm, luminous, and energising — our brand presence should feel like stepping into good light |
| **Honest** | We do not oversell. We speak plainly about ingredients, results, and limitations — because our customers are intelligent |
| **Culturally Rooted** | We celebrate Southeast Asian identity without tokenising it; our cultural references are specific, not generic |
| **Progressive** | We are ahead of trends, not behind them. We speak to where beauty is going, not where it has been |
| **Inclusive** | Beauty at Lumière Collective has no default. Our imagery, language, and KOL choices reflect the full spectrum of the communities we serve |

## Brand Mission, Vision & Values

### Mission
To make premium, clean, and culturally resonant beauty accessible to every person in Southeast Asia — through honest products, authentic partnerships, and a community built on trust.

### Vision
To become Southeast Asia's defining premium DTC beauty brand by 2028 — the brand that the next generation of SEA consumers chooses as their first introduction to luxury skincare.

### Values

| Value | Operational Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Formulation Integrity** | No ingredient compromise for cost. Every formula is reviewed by our independent R&D board before approval. |
| **Community Before Campaign** | Our KOL partnerships are long-term relationships, not one-off transactions. We invest in creators who share our values. |
| **Transparency as Default** | We publish full ingredient lists, disclose all paid partnerships, and do not make unsubstantiated clinical claims. |
| **Halal as a Standard, Not a Segment** | Our entire product range is formulated to halal compliance standards — not as a marketing angle, but as the minimum bar for ingredient integrity. |
| **SEA First** | Every product development, marketing, and community decision begins with "does this serve our SEA customer?" |

## Tone of Voice & Messaging Framework

### Core Tone Principles

| Principle | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Clear over clever** | "This serum visibly reduces dark spots in 4 weeks" | "Illuminate your luminosity journey" |
| **Warm but not effusive** | "We think you'll love this one." | "OMG YOU NEED THIS IN YOUR LIFE 🤩🤩🤩" |
| **Specific over vague** | "68% of users saw reduced pores after 3 weeks of daily use" | "Transforms skin dramatically" |
| **Culturally specific** | Reference Tết, Songkran, Eid in ways that are specific and genuine | Generic "festival season" references |
| **Community-inclusive** | "For every skin tone, every routine, every budget tier that reaches for premium" | "For everyone" (meaningless) |

### Messaging Hierarchy

**Level 1 — Brand Message (all touchpoints):**
Lumière Collective is premium beauty made honest, accessible, and proudly SEA.

**Level 2 — Product Message (category level):**
- Glow Serum Series: "Clinically graded brightening for real SEA skin."
- Velvet Cushion Collection: "Full-coverage finish. Feather-light wear. All-day in humidity."
- Clean Ritual Body Care: "Your body deserves the same care as your face."

**Level 3 — Campaign Message (Q2 2025 "Glow Forward"):**
"Glow isn't a finish line. It's the direction you're moving."

- **Headlines:** Playfair Display (serif) — editorial authority, premium presence
- **Body Copy:** DM Sans (sans-serif) — readability, digital-native clarity
- **Accent/Campaign:** Cormorant Garamond Italic — luxury moments, tagline applications

### Photography Standards
- **Skin representation:** Minimum 60% of all campaign imagery to feature Southeast Asian skin tones (warm undertones, Type III–V Fitzpatrick scale)
- **Texture and realism:** Avoid over-retouching. Visible pores, natural skin texture, and real-life lighting are preferred over airbrushed perfection
- **Background:** White, soft-focus natural environments, or brand palette neutrals. Avoid busy or unrelated settings
- **Product isolation shots:** Must show product on skin or in hand — never floating on artificial gradient backgrounds

## Content Guidelines by Platform

### TikTok
- **Format:** Short video (15–60s) as primary; mid-form (61–180s) for tutorials
- **Hook requirement:** First 3 seconds must establish the problem, transformation, or product clearly — no slow build-ups
- **Caption:** Maximum 150 characters; include 1 branded hashtag (#GlowForward or #LumiereCollective) + 2 trending category hashtags
- **Posting cadence (brand-owned):** 4–5x per week; peak posting times 7–9am and 8–10pm local market
- **Music:** Use trending audio where possible; avoid licensed commercial music without clearance
- **TikTok Shop integration:** All campaign posts must include a product tag linked to TikTok Shop where available (VN, ID, MY, TH)

### Instagram
- **Primary formats:** Reels (priority), Carousel (product education), Static (brand lifestyle)
- **Reels:** 30–90s; hook within first 2 seconds; subtitles mandatory (60% of viewers watch without sound)
- **Grid aesthetic:** Maintain a balanced ratio of product-focused (40%), lifestyle (35%), and community/UGC (25%) posts
- **Stories:** Daily cadence during campaign windows; poll + Q&A mechanics to drive engagement; product link stickers on all campaign posts
- **Caption style:** Conversational opening sentence; expand with product detail; end with a soft CTA ("Swipe to shop" or "Link in bio")
- **Hashtag limit:** 5–8 hashtags per post (avoid hashtag stacking)

### YouTube
- **Format:** Long-form reviews (10–20 min), tutorial series (8–15 min), brand mini-documentaries (3–6 min)
- **Thumbnail standard:** Face + product visible; emotion (curiosity or satisfaction); readable text overlay in Playfair Display
- **Description:** First 200 characters to include product names, key claims, and affiliate/discount link
- **End screen:** Must include subscription CTA and link to 1 related Lumière Collective video
- **Ideal publishing cadence:** 1–2 videos per month per creator; quality over frequency

### Zalo (Vietnam) / LINE (Thailand)
- **Role:** Post-purchase nurture, exclusive offers, community-building
- **Tone:** More personal and conversational than other platforms — closer to a friend's message
- **Content:** Early access to launches, loyalty rewards, skincare tips, replenishment reminders
- **KOL integration:** Share KOL content links within brand channels; avoid direct-to-KOL-profile linking that takes users off-platform

### Content Approval Process

**Step 1 — Brief Delivery**
Lumière Collective delivers a detailed creative brief including: campaign narrative, key messages (mandatory and optional), product claims approved for use, visual references, prohibited content list, and disclosure requirements. Deadline for KOL acknowledgment: 48 hours.

**Step 2 — Draft Review**
KOL submits draft content (video, script, or storyboard) for brand review. Review turnaround: 3 business days. Lumière Collective may request up to 2 rounds of revision; substantive changes to key messages require brand team sign-off.

**Step 3 — Final Approval & Publishing**
Final approved content is logged in the content management system. KOL may not publish without written confirmation from the Lumière Collective campaign manager. Post-publication, KOL must share live URL within 2 hours; brand team monitors for disclosure compliance.

### Usage Rights & Exclusivity Terms

- **Usage rights:** Lumière Collective retains the right to repurpose KOL content across owned channels (brand Instagram, TikTok, brand website, email) for 12 months post-publication, without additional fee, unless otherwise negotiated.
- **Exclusivity — Standard:** KOLs may not post content for direct competitor beauty brands in the same product category within 30 days before or after Lumière Collective campaign posting dates.
- **Exclusivity — Premium tier:** Macro and Mega KOLs on 12-month partnerships operate under a 90-day category exclusivity window.
- **Competitive categories:** Skincare serums, cushion foundations, body care, and any product directly overlapping with Lumière Collective's flagship lines.

### Payment Structure

| Model | Application | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Flat Fee | All tiers; base compensation for content creation | Agreed per deliverable (post, Reel, YouTube video) |
| Performance Bonus | Mid-tier to Macro | Tiered bonus on GMV attribution above agreed threshold |
| Affiliate Commission | Nano and Micro supplement | 8–12% commission on tracked sales via unique code |
| Product Gifting | Nano only | Non-monetary; product value equivalent to deliverable value |

## Ideal KOL Characteristics

Lumière Collective seeks KOL partners who embody the following profile:

1. **Authentic skincare practice** — The creator genuinely uses and understands skincare. Formulated product knowledge and real usage routines are visible in their content history.
2. **Audience demographic alignment** — Primary audience falls within 18–38 female demographic (or male grooming segment for relevant campaigns) in one or more of Lumière Collective's five markets.
3. **Content consistency** — Posts at a regular cadence with consistent visual quality. No unexplained dormancy periods of 30+ days in the prior 6 months.
4. **Positive community sentiment** — Comment section reflects genuine engagement. Brand mentions in comments are predominantly positive.
5. **Brand value alignment** — Demonstrable alignment with at least two of Lumière Collective's three pillars: Clean Beauty, Cultural Pride, or Accessible Luxury.
6. **Disclosure-compliant history** — No prior violations of advertising disclosure regulations in their operating market.
7. **Platform authenticity signals** — Engagement rate consistent with follower count; no evidence of purchased followers or engagement pods.

## Unsuitable Influencer Profiles

The following characteristics disqualify a creator from Lumière Collective collaboration consideration, regardless of follower count or engagement metrics:

### Content Category Conflicts
- Creators whose primary content involves tobacco, alcohol, gambling, or vaping products
- Creators who produce content that promotes restrictive eating, extreme diet culture, or body image messaging inconsistent with Lumière Collective's inclusive beauty values
- Creators whose content includes explicit, adult-rated, or sexually suggestive material
- Creators whose content platform is primarily gaming, sports, or automotive with no established lifestyle/beauty presence

### Audience Demographic Mismatches
- Creators whose audience is more than 80% outside Lumière Collective's five operating markets
- Creators whose audience skews above 45 years old (60%+ in this cohort) unless specifically targeting premium/luxury segment campaigns
- Creators whose male audience exceeds 75% — unless engaged specifically for a male grooming campaign

### Platform Behavior Red Flags
- Engagement rate more than 60% below tier benchmark (likely purchased engagement)
- Follower growth spikes of more than 30% in a single month without an identifiable viral event
- Follower-to-engagement ratio inconsistencies flagged by Social Blade or equivalent audit tools
- Comment sections dominated by bot patterns, emoji-only responses, or generic praise without specific content reference
- History of undisclosed advertising in any market in the prior 12 months

### Competitive Brand Conflicts
- Active paid partnerships with brands in the following competitive categories: luxury skincare serums, premium cushion foundations, mass-to-premium DTC beauty brands operating in SEA, body care brands with direct SKU overlap
- Exclusive contracts with major competing platforms that restrict content publishing rights

- "Removes dark spots in X days" — Therapeutic claim; not approved ❌
- "Prescribed by dermatologists" — Misleading clinical authority ❌
- "Suitable for all skin types without exceptions" — Overgeneralization ❌
- "100% natural" — Inaccurate; formulation includes lab-synthesised actives ❌
- Any claim implying medical treatment or drug-equivalent efficacy ❌

### KOL Messaging Requirements (Non-Negotiable)
1. Product name must be stated clearly at least once in video or caption
2. Disclosure hashtag must appear within the first 3 lines of caption
3. At least one product benefit claim must align with an approved claim from the list above
4. Halal certification status must be mentioned for any content targeting MY or ID Muslim audiences

## Platform-Specific Communication Guidelines

### TikTok Tone Adaptation
Conversational, peer-level, trend-aware. KOLs should speak as they would to a friend, not as a spokesperson. Avoid scripted language. First-person experience ("I've been using this for two weeks and...") outperforms third-person product description. Challenge mechanics and POV formats are strongly encouraged.

### Instagram Tone Adaptation
Elevated but approachable. Slightly more editorial than TikTok. Captions can be longer and more reflective — Instagram's audience expects considered content. Beauty aesthetic should align with Lumière Collective's visual palette (muted, warm, clean). Over-saturated or high-contrast editing should be avoided.

### YouTube Tone Adaptation
Informative, honest, thorough. YouTube audiences are self-selecting for depth — they want to understand the product, not just be sold it. KOLs should cover: first impressions, usage protocol, 2–4 week results, and honest limitations. Sponsored content should be clearly identified early in the video but need not interrupt the content's natural flow.

## Prohibited Content Categories

The following content categories are never permitted in Lumière Collective-branded or Lumière Collective-associated influencer content, regardless of market, campaign, or creator:

- Any content that body-shames, promotes eating disorders, or implies beauty requires a specific weight or body shape
- Content mocking, stereotyping, or othering any ethnic, religious, or cultural group
- Sexual or suggestive content featuring minors
- Content involving political commentary or endorsement of political parties or candidates
- Misleading before-and-after imagery (must include disclosure if filtered or edited)
- Content that makes unverified medical claims
- Any use of unauthorised music, font, image, or video content (copyright compliance is creator's responsibility)

## Crisis Communication Guidelines

In the event of a brand-impacting incident involving a KOL partner (controversy, viral negative content, disclosed misconduct), the following escalation protocol applies:

**Level 1 — Minor Incident** (e.g., single negative comment, minor brand inconsistency)
Response: Monitor; no public action required. Internal note logged.

**Level 2 — Moderate Incident** (e.g., KOL posts content inconsistent with brand values; minor platform violation)
Response: Private outreach to KOL within 24 hours; content removal request; content correction within 48 hours. No public statement.

**Level 3 — Major Incident** (e.g., KOL associated with hate speech, serious ethical violation, legal issue)
Response: Immediate posting pause on all collaborative content. Internal review within 48 hours. Content deletion requested. Partnership suspension pending review. Public statement only if brand directly implicated in press coverage.

All crisis response actions must be approved by the VP of Brand before external communication.

## Compliance & Legal Requirements

### Advertising Disclosure (All Markets)
All paid partnership content must comply with market-specific disclosure standards detailed in the Influencer Collaboration Policy section. Non-compliant posts are the creator's legal responsibility under local consumer protection law, but brand association risk requires Lumière Collective to ensure compliance at the contract level.

### Halal Certification
All product claims referencing halal compliance must use the phrase "JAKIM-certified halal formulation" (MY) or "MUI-certified halal formulation" (ID) where applicable. Generic "halal-friendly" language without certification reference is not approved.

### Health Claims
No influencer content may make therapeutic, medical, or clinical claims that exceed the approved claims list in the Campaign Messaging Standards section. Any claim suggesting the product treats, cures, or prevents a medical condition (acne, eczema, rosacea, etc.) is prohibited unless pre-approved with supporting clinical trial documentation.

### Data Privacy
KOL collaboration data (contact details, contract terms, payment information) is subject to Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Decree 13/2023 and applicable market-equivalent legislation. KOL contact databases may not be shared outside the Partnerships and Marketing teams without Legal approval.
