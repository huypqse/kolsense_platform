## Executive Summary

Southeast Asia's beauty and personal care market is projected to reach **USD 28.4 billion by 2027**, growing at a CAGR of 6.8% driven by rising disposable incomes, digital-native consumer cohorts, and the accelerating influence of social commerce on purchase behaviour. For Lumière Collective, the "Glow Forward" Q2 2025 campaign planning cycle required a comprehensive re-evaluation of audience composition, platform dynamics, and content preferences across all five operating markets.

This report identifies five distinct consumer segments that collectively define Lumière Collective's addressable market: Gen Z Beauty Enthusiasts, Millennial Self-Care Seekers, Working Professional Premium Buyers, Muslim Beauty Consumers, and Male Grooming Adopters. Across all segments, three structural trends stand out as campaign-defining forces:

1. **TikTok Shop social commerce** is fundamentally restructuring the beauty purchase journey in VN, ID, and MY — blurring the line between content discovery and transaction.
2. **Ingredient literacy** is rising sharply among the 25–34 cohort, increasing demand for evidence-led influencer content over purely aesthetic posts.
3. **Halal beauty** has evolved from a niche to a mainstream category signal across MY, ID, and increasingly SG — presenting a cross-market growth opportunity that Lumière Collective is well-positioned to capture.

## Audience Segmentation

### Segment 1: Gen Z Beauty Enthusiasts (18–24)

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Size (% of TAM) | 31% |
| Avg Monthly Beauty Spend | USD 28–55 |
| Platform Preference | TikTok (1st) · Instagram (2nd) · YouTube (3rd) |
| Preferred Content Formats | Short video (<60s), GRWM, challenge/duet, before-and-after |
| Key Purchase Triggers | Viral content, peer recommendations, discount codes, limited editions |
| Trust Signals | KOL demo in real skin, UGC, TikTok comment reactions |
| Top Markets | VN, ID, MY |

Gen Z beauty consumers in SEA are platform-native deal hunters who balance aspirational brand awareness with price sensitivity. They are the primary driver of TikTok Shop adoption and show the highest responsiveness to challenge-format content (3.2x higher engagement vs. editorial). Brand discovery is almost exclusively social-first; search and brand websites play a negligible awareness role.

### Segment 2: Millennial Self-Care Seekers (25–34)

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Size (% of TAM) | 34% |
| Avg Monthly Beauty Spend | USD 62–140 |
| Platform Preference | Instagram (1st) · YouTube (2nd) · TikTok (3rd) |
| Preferred Content Formats | Long-form YouTube reviews, Instagram tutorials, "skincare investment" editorial |
| Key Purchase Triggers | Ingredient transparency, clinical claims, KOL credibility, "worth it" reviews |
| Trust Signals | Dermatologist endorsement, ingredient deep-dives, before-and-after over 30+ days |
| Top Markets | TH, SG, VN |

Millennials are Lumière Collective's highest-GMV segment per buyer. They purchase with greater deliberation, conduct multi-platform research, and respond to evidence-based content narratives. YouTube is particularly influential at the consideration and conversion stages for this segment — a 2024 Meta study found that 68% of SEA Millennial beauty buyers watched at least one YouTube review before purchasing a premium skincare product above USD 30. They exhibit the highest brand loyalty of any segment once trust is established.

### Segment 3: Working Professionals — Premium Buyers (30–40)

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Size (% of TAM) | 18% |
| Avg Monthly Beauty Spend | USD 120–280 |
| Platform Preference | Instagram (1st) · YouTube (2nd) · LinkedIn (supplementary) |
| Preferred Content Formats | Editorial reviews, "daily routine" lifestyle content, long-form tutorials |
| Key Purchase Triggers | Time-efficiency, clinical efficacy, professional recommendations, brand heritage |

This segment drives the highest average order values in the Lumière Collective customer base (USD 38–62 per transaction) and responds strongly to the credibility signals associated with Priya Subramaniam's content style. They are increasingly interested in "cosmeceutical" positioning (the intersection of cosmetics and clinical efficacy) and have the lowest price sensitivity of any segment. Time scarcity is a key behavioural driver — streamlined purchasing experiences (saved payment, auto-replenishment) significantly reduce churn.

A 2024 Euromonitor analysis estimated that the SEA halal beauty market reached USD 2.8 billion, with Malaysia and Indonesia accounting for 78% of spend. KOL authenticity is paramount — consumers in this segment are highly sensitive to perceived "token" halal endorsements vs. genuine community voices.

The category is currently underfunded by premium DTC brands relative to its growth trajectory — Lumière Collective has first-mover advantage in this space through the "Clean Ritual Body Care" line and Rizky Aditya's established audience.

## Cross-Platform Behavior Analysis

SEA beauty consumers are emphatically multi-platform. A TikTok for Business SEA 2025 study found that 74% of beauty purchase decisions involve content from at least two different platforms. The most common journey patterns observed in Lumière Collective's Q2 2025 attribution data:

| Journey Pattern | Frequency | Markets |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok Discovery → TikTok Shop Purchase | 34% | VN, ID, MY |
| TikTok Discovery → Brand Website Purchase | 18% | All |
| Instagram Discovery → YouTube Research → Purchase | 14% | TH, SG |
| YouTube Research → Instagram Browsing → Purchase | 11% | SG, TH |
| Facebook Retargeting → Website Purchase | 9% | VN, ID |
| Instagram Discovery → Shopee/Lazada Purchase | 8% | ID, MY |
| Other/Direct | 6% | All |

The TikTok-to-TikTok Shop pattern (34%) represents the highest single-journey concentration and reflects the effectiveness of the platform's closed-loop social commerce infrastructure. For Q3 planning, closing the loop on the Instagram Discovery → YouTube Research journey (14%) with a coordinated post-YouTube call-to-action strategy is recommended.

## Beauty Category Deep-Dives

### Skincare (Hero Category for Lumière Collective)

- **SEA Market Size (2025):** USD 11.2 billion
- **Top Consumer Pain Points:** Hyperpigmentation (VN, TH), acne and excess sebum (ID, MY), sensitivity to humid climate, sun damage/SPF compliance gap
- **Preferred KOL Content Style:** Before-and-after documentation over 4+ weeks; ingredient deep-dives; "I tried X for 30 days" format; dermatologist guest appearances
- **Purchase Channel Preference:** Brand website/app (SG, TH — trust in authenticity); TikTok Shop (VN, ID — convenience); Shopee/Lazada (MY, ID — price comparison)
- **Growth Opportunity:** SPF + brightening as a gateway category; consumers who enter via SPF convert to multi-step routine SKUs at 2.4x rate within 6 months

### Color Cosmetics

- **SEA Market Size (2025):** USD 6.8 billion
- **Top Consumer Pain Points:** Longevity in humid climate, oxidation on darker skin tones, limited shade diversity for South/Southeast Asian skin tones
- **Preferred KOL Content Style:** GRWM, OOTD integration, color swatch content, "does it transfer?" test formats
- **Purchase Channel Preference:** TikTok Shop (Gen Z impulse), Sephora/Guardian (try-before-buy for premium), online marketplace (value tier)
- **Growth Opportunity:** "Blush-forward" and glass-skin makeup aesthetics are surging among 18–24 cohort in VN and TH — content-first launch strategy recommended

### Halal Beauty

- **SEA Market Size (2025):** USD 3.1 billion (MY + ID core; growing in SG)
- **Top Consumer Pain Points:** Difficulty verifying halal status, limited availability of premium certified options, distrust of "halal-washed" brands
- **Preferred KOL Content Style:** "Halal check" ingredient reviews, certification explainer content, modest GRWM
- **Purchase Channel Preference:** TikTok Shop (MY, ID), Shopee (MY), brand website for premium certification assurance
- **Growth Opportunity:** Premium halal beauty is undersupplied relative to demand. Consumers willing to pay a 15–25% premium for certified-premium products vs. uncertified mass alternatives.

### Men's Grooming

- **SEA Market Size (2025):** USD 2.4 billion (growing at 12.1% CAGR)
- **Top Consumer Pain Points:** "Too complicated" skincare routines, lack of male-coded branding, price sensitivity, stigma in older age cohorts
- **Preferred KOL Content Style:** "Simple 3-step routine," honest review, sports/gym lifestyle integration, "your girlfriend will notice" social proof framing (for 18–24)
- **Purchase Channel Preference:** TikTok Shop (18–24), marketplace (25–34 value seekers), brand website (premium adopters)
- **Growth Opportunity:** The 22–30 male cohort in Indonesia and Vietnam is the highest-growth entry segment. Functional minimalism (fewer products, clear results) is the winning content and product strategy.

## Business Recommendations for Influencer Marketing

1. **Adopt a segment-first briefing framework** — Brief KOLs by audience segment (Gen Z, Millennial, Halal, Male), not just by platform. Minh Châu's Gen Z brief should differ from Nattaya's Millennial brief even when both are posting on the same platform.

2. **Invest in TikTok Shop as primary commerce infrastructure for VN, ID, and MY** — The 34% direct TikTok Shop purchase journey, combined with the lowest session-to-purchase drop-off rate, makes TikTok Shop the highest-ROI commerce channel for these markets. Q3 budget should allocate a minimum of 40% to TikTok (up from 38%).

3. **Develop an ingredient transparency content series** — Ingredient literacy is rising fastest in the Millennial and Premium segments (TH, SG). A "What's In It" creator series, led by Priya Subramaniam or Nattaya Siriporn, would capture this demand and strengthen Lumière Collective's "clean beauty" credibility.

4. **Build a halal beauty sub-brand content strategy** — Lumière Collective's existing halal certification is an underutilised asset. A dedicated halal beauty content series with Aisyah Binte Razak and 2–3 additional MY/ID micro-influencers could address the 26% Muslim consumer segment with minimal incremental production cost.

5. **Invest in post-purchase Zalo and LINE nurture** — The 18.2% 30-day repurchase rate from "Glow Forward" demonstrates that post-purchase engagement drives meaningful LTV uplift. Scaling Zalo (VN) and LINE (TH) CRM sequences for KOL-attributed customers is the highest-ROI retention investment available.

6. **Explore TikTok Shop Thailand launch** — Thailand's TikTok Shop penetration (39%) is growing rapidly. Nattaya Siriporn's Instagram-first Thai audience contains a large TikTok-active cohort that could be activated through a coordinated TikTok Shop launch in Q3.
