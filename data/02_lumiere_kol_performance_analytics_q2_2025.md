## Analyst Evaluation Comments

**Minh Châu Trần — Analyst Note (Senior Influencer Strategist):**
Châu consistently outperforms contract expectations. Her 7-day challenge mechanic in Q2 2025 was organically adopted by her audience without paid amplification, generating UGC that extended the campaign's effective reach by an estimated 18%. She is Lumière Collective's highest-priority retention target for Q3 and H2 2025. We recommend formalising a 12-month partnership framework with quarterly activation rights and a right-of-first-refusal clause on competitor beauty category campaigns.

**Nattaya Siriporn — Analyst Note (Thailand Market Lead):**
Nattaya delivers premium positioning that no other asset on our current roster can replicate in Thailand. Her audience's willingness to engage with long-form YouTube content (avg 58% view duration) signals a high-consideration buyer that converts at premium AOV. The trade-off is production cadence — she should not be briefed on campaigns with less than 3 weeks lead time. For Q3, we recommend co-creating a "Skincare Founder" YouTube series connecting her directly with Lumière Collective's product development team.

**Rizky Aditya — Analyst Note (Indonesia Market Lead):**
Rizky's "Glow Forward" performance validated our hypothesis that male grooming is a viable growth segment for Lumière Collective in Indonesia. His YouTube comment section is worth monitoring as a qualitative insights source — we identified three product feature requests from his audience that have been surfaced to the product team. Recommend expanding his brief in Q3 to include the Clean Ritual Body Care line, which has strong gender-neutral positioning potential.

**Priya Subramaniam — Analyst Note (SEA Regional Strategist):**
Priya's value lies outside standard GMV metrics. Her content functions as third-party brand validation for the regional premium segment — Lumière Collective's brand search volume in SG increased 22% in the 4 weeks following her "Glow Forward" review. We recommend investing in a quarterly editorial partnership that prioritises brand equity building over short-term conversion, with a 6-month exclusivity clause to lock out competitor luxury brands.

**Aisyah Binte Razak — Analyst Note (Malaysia Market Lead):**
Aisyah is the most efficient investment on the Q2 2025 roster by any cost-normalised metric. At USD 1,500 total fee, her ROAS of 4.6x represents exceptional ROI. The recommendation is to significantly expand her retainer for H2 2025 and use her as the anchor for a dedicated halal beauty sub-campaign targeting both MY and ID Muslim consumers. Her audience's cross-border reach in Indonesia (18% of audience) creates a compelling multi-market value proposition that is currently undermonetised.
