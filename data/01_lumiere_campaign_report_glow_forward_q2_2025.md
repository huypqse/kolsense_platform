## Executive Summary

The "Glow Forward" Q2 2025 campaign was Lumière Collective's most ambitious influencer-led activation to date, spanning five Southeast Asian markets — Vietnam (VN), Thailand (TH), Indonesia (ID), Singapore (SG), and Malaysia (MY). The campaign deployed a roster of five carefully selected Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook, with the primary objective of driving brand awareness among premium beauty consumers aged 18–34 and accelerating direct-to-consumer (DTC) conversions.

Over the 13-week campaign window, Lumière Collective achieved a total Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) of **USD 374,200**, a Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) of **4.6x**, and a blended Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) of **USD 7.40**. Total campaign impressions exceeded **48 million** across all channels, with TikTok emerging as the dominant performance platform by both reach and conversion efficiency.

Key highlights:
- TikTok drove **61%** of total attributed GMV despite representing only **38%** of total budget
- Minh Châu Trần delivered the highest single-creator ROAS at **5.8x** in the Vietnamese market
- The campaign overperformed on the 25–34 age segment, achieving a **34%** share vs. a planned **28%**
- Halal beauty sub-campaign (led by Aisyah Binte Razak) achieved a **4.1% click-to-purchase conversion rate**, highest across all content verticals

## Campaign Strategy

The "Glow Forward" campaign was built on a **Funnel Amplification Model**, designed to leverage different KOL tiers and content formats at each stage of the consumer decision journey.

### Strategic Framework

**Awareness Layer (Top of Funnel)**
Short-form TikTok and Instagram Reels content from macro-influencers Minh Châu Trần and Rizky Aditya seeded broad reach and category curiosity. Content format: GRWM (Get Ready With Me), morning skincare routines, before-and-after demonstrations.

**Consideration Layer (Mid Funnel)**
YouTube long-form reviews from Nattaya Siriporn and Priya Subramaniam provided deep product education and independent credibility signals to audiences in higher-consideration markets (TH, SG). Average video length: 12–18 minutes.

**Conversion Layer (Bottom of Funnel)**
TikTok Shop integrations and Instagram affiliate links with exclusive discount codes ("GLOW15") created frictionless conversion pathways. Aisyah Binte Razak's halal-certified product emphasis drove strong conversion in the MY market.

**Retention Layer**
Post-purchase Zalo (VN) and LINE (TH) nurture sequences were triggered for KOL-attributed customers, with a 30-day repurchase rate of **18.2%** achieved.

## Campaign Insights & Key Learnings

### What Worked
1. **TikTok Shop frictionless commerce** — In-app purchasing removed the drop-off risk of redirecting users to an external site. VN and ID TikTok Shop conversion rates were 2.1x higher than Instagram link-in-bio conversion.
2. **Challenge content format** — Minh Châu Trần's 7-day challenge drove sustained engagement over an extended period vs. single-post decay. Organic shares contributed 31% of total reach on this content piece.
3. **Male grooming as emerging vertical** — Rizky Aditya's inclusion expanded Lumière Collective's addressable market in ID by an estimated 12%. First-time male purchasers accounted for 9.4% of total new customers.
4. **Halal micro-influencer efficiency** — Aisyah Binte Razak's USD 1,500 total fee generated USD 22,200 GMV, a ROAS of 4.6x — comparable to the macro-influencer average at a fraction of the investment.
5. **Paid amplification gating** — Boosting only posts that cleared organic performance thresholds reduced wasted spend and increased overall paid ROAS by an estimated 0.8x vs. a blanket boost strategy.

### What Underperformed
1. **YouTube direct conversion** — Despite strong view duration and engagement metrics, YouTube attribution to direct GMV was lower than anticipated (3.5% budget → 11.8% GMV). The platform's contribution to assisted conversions was not fully captured in this cycle.
2. **Facebook engagement in MY** — Facebook posts in Malaysia showed the weakest engagement across the campaign (1.4% avg), indicating platform irrelevance for the MY audience and suggesting budget should be reallocated.
3. **35–44 audience reach** — This segment underperformed vs. plan, suggesting current KOL roster skews younger. Consideration should be given to adding a creator with stronger resonance in this cohort for Q3.

## Business Recommendations

### Immediate Actions (Q3 2025)
1. **Expand TikTok Shop to Thailand** — TikTok Shop is live in VN, ID, and MY but not yet activated for TH. Given Nattaya Siriporn's strong Instagram performance in TH, a coordinated TikTok Shop launch in Q3 with her involvement is recommended. Projected incremental GMV: USD 40,000–60,000.
2. **Re-engage Minh Châu Trần for a Q3 exclusive** — Her 5.8x ROAS and 10,820 new customers make her the highest-value KOL on the roster. A Q3 exclusivity arrangement with 15% fee uplift is commercially justified.
3. **Invest in YouTube attribution tracking** — Implement UTM-tagged YouTube description links and post-purchase attribution surveys to capture YouTube's true contribution to the conversion journey before reducing its budget allocation.

### Strategic Recommendations (H2 2025)
4. **Build a Male Grooming sub-brand campaign** — The male audience response to Rizky Aditya's content suggests sufficient market demand for a dedicated male grooming campaign in Indonesia, leveraging the "Clean Ritual Body Care" line. Target Q4 2025.
5. **Scale halal beauty micro-influencer network** — At a 4.6x ROAS and sub-USD 2,000 fee, halal micro-influencers represent the highest-efficiency segment in the portfolio. Recommend recruiting 3–5 additional halal beauty creators in MY and ID for Q3.
6. **Develop a Priya Subramaniam "luxury editorial" series** — Her SG audience demonstrates the highest average order value (USD 38.10) and premium product affinity. A quarterly long-form YouTube + Instagram editorial partnership would strengthen Lumière Collective's luxury positioning in SG.
7. **Discontinue Facebook as KOL content channel** — Redirect the USD ~8,900 Facebook budget to TikTok paid amplification and YouTube pre-roll in VN and ID, markets where Facebook's audience is already reachable via the younger-skewing platforms.
